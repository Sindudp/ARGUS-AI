ARGUS INCIDENT RESPONSE PLAYBOOK

**Incident Response Playbook**

**Threat 1: Brute Force Attack**

1. **IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (first 15 minutes)**:
	* Block the source IP address 192.168.1.105 at the firewall to prevent further login attempts.
	* Disable the targeted admin account to prevent potential unauthorized access.
	* Isolate the affected system (10.0.0.1) from the network to prevent lateral movement.
	* Alert the security team and relevant stakeholders of the potential security incident.
2. **CONTAINMENT PLAYBOOK (step by step numbered list)**:
	1. **SOC analyst**: Verify the incident and confirm the brute force attack (Time estimate: 5 minutes).
	2. **System admin**: Change the password of the targeted admin account and ensure that all accounts have robust passwords (Time estimate: 10 minutes).
	3. **SOC analyst**: Perform a thorough analysis of the login attempt logs to identify potential vulnerabilities (Time estimate: 30 minutes).
	4. **System admin**: Implement additional security measures, such as multi-factor authentication and rate limiting on login attempts (Time estimate: 60 minutes).
	5. **Management**: Notify stakeholders and provide updates on the incident (Time estimate: Ongoing).
3. **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (non-technical, 3 sentences)**:
	A brute force attack was detected, where an attacker attempted to guess the password of an admin account. The attack was blocked, and the affected account was disabled to prevent potential unauthorized access. The security team is working to implement additional security measures to prevent similar incidents in the future.
4. **SEVERITY RATING**: CRITICAL
5. **ESTIMATED RECOVERY TIME**: 2 hours
