## 1️⃣ PLAYBOOK – RANSOMWARE_SPREAD  
**Severity:** 95 | **MITRE ATT&CK:** T1021 – Remote Services (Lateral Movement)

### Management Summary (2 sentences)  
Ransomware has begun propagating laterally across the environment by using compromised valid accounts to log into remote services (RDP/SSH/VNC). Immediate containment of remote‑service access and rapid isolation of infected hosts are required to stop further encryption.

### Immediate Actions (first ≤ 30 min)  
- **Block** all inbound RDP/SSH/VNC traffic at the perimeter firewall except from approved jump‑hosts.  
- **Disable** the compromised user accounts identified in the logs.  
- **Activate** the “Network‑Isolation” playbook on any host that shows ransomware execution alerts.

### Containment & Eradication Steps  

| # | Action | Owner | Target Completion |
|---|--------|-------|--------------------|
| 1 | Pull the latest list of active remote‑service sessions from domain controllers and SIEM. | SOC Analyst | **15 min** |
| 2 | Correlate sessions with the compromised accounts and create a block list. | Threat Intel Lead | **30 min** |
| 3 | Push firewall/NSG deny rules for the block list across all sub‑nets. | Network Engineer | **45 min** |
| 4 | Quarantine each host that has an active ransomware process (identified by the EDR alert) – disconnect from LAN and disable network adapters. | Incident Commander + Endpoint Team | **1 hr** |
| 5 | Capture volatile memory and disk images from the quarantined hosts for forensic analysis. | Forensics Lead | **2 hr** |
| 6 | Reset passwords for all accounts that were used for remote‑service logins and enforce MFA on every remote‑service account. | IAM Administrator | **4 hr** |
| 7 | Conduct a “golden‑image” rebuild of each infected host (re‑image from known clean baseline). | IT Operations | **8 hr** |
| 8 | Validate that no new remote‑service sessions appear for the compromised accounts for 24 h (continuous monitoring). | SOC Analyst | **24 h** |
| 9 | Update remote‑service hardening standards (disable unused services, enforce network‑level authentication, restrict source IPs). | Security Architect | **48 h** |

## 2️⃣ PLAYBOOK – DATA_EXFILTRATION  
**Severity:** 85 | **MITRE ATT&CK:** T1041 – Exfiltration Over Command‑and‑Control Channel

### Management Summary (2 sentences)  
Sensitive data has been staged and exfiltrated by encoding it into an existing C2 channel, bypassing traditional perimeter controls. Stopping the outbound flow and tightening data‑loss‑prevention controls are essential to prevent further loss.

### Immediate Actions (first ≤ 30 min)  
- **Shut down** the identified C2 outbound connection (block the destination IP/hostname).  
- **Enable** DLP alerts on all high‑value data repositories.  
- **Notify** the legal/compliance team of a potential data breach.

### Containment & Eradication Steps  

| # | Action | Owner | Target Completion |
|---|--------|-------|--------------------|
| 1 | Extract the C2 IP/hostname from the network flow logs that carried the exfiltrated payload. | SOC Analyst | **15 min** |
| 2 | Add a deny rule for that IP/hostname on the perimeter firewall and on internal proxy devices. | Network Engineer | **30 min** |
| 3 | Identify all hosts that communicated with the C2 endpoint in the last 24 h. | Threat Intel Lead | **45 min** |
| 4 | Isolate those hosts from the corporate network (quarantine VLAN). | Incident Commander | **1 hr** |
| 5 | Collect full packet captures and host memory for each isolated system. | Forensics Lead | **2 hr** |
| 6 | Run a DLP scan on the affected hosts to locate any residual staged data. | DLP Engineer | **4 hr** |
| 7 | Remove any malicious scripts or tools used to encode data into the C2 channel (based on forensic findings). | Endpoint Team | **6 hr** |
| 8 | Reset credentials for any accounts that were used to access the compromised data stores. | IAM Administrator | **8 hr** |
| 9 | Deploy a network‑traffic anomaly detection rule (e.g., high‑entropy payloads on allowed ports) and monitor for 72 h. | SOC Analyst | **24 h** |
|10 | Update outbound‑traffic filtering policies to require proxy inspection for all non‑web protocols. | Security Architect | **48 h** |

## 3️⃣ PLAYBOOK – RANSOMWARE_START  
**Severity:** 80 | **MITRE ATT&CK:** T1486 – Data Encrypted for Impact

### Management Summary (2 sentences)  
Ransomware has been executed on a host, encrypting files and rendering them inaccessible. Rapid isolation, forensic capture, and restoration from clean backups are required to limit operational impact.

### Immediate Actions (first ≤ 30 min)  
- **Disconnect** the infected endpoint from the network (disable NIC or place in quarantine VLAN).  
- **Stop** the ransomware process via the endpoint detection platform.  
- **Alert** the Business Continuity team to prepare for potential service restoration.

### Containment & Eradication Steps  

| # | Action | Owner | Target Completion |
|---|--------|-------|--------------------|
| 1 | Pull the EDR alert details (process name, hash, command line). | SOC Analyst | **10 min** |
| 2 | Issue a “kill‑process” command to terminate the ransomware executable on the infected host. | Endpoint Team | **20 min** |
| 3 | Capture a forensic image of the host’s disk and memory before any further changes. | Forensics Lead | **1 hr** |
| 4 | Verify whether shadow copies have been deleted; if so, note for recovery scope. | Backup Engineer | **2 hr** |
| 5 | Restore the affected systems from the most recent clean offline backup. | IT Operations | **4 hr** |
| 6 | Apply the latest security patches and enable application‑whitelisting on the restored hosts. | Patch Management | **6 hr** |
| 7 | Re‑enable network connectivity only after confirming the host is clean (no ransomware processes, no anomalous scheduled tasks). | Incident Commander | **8 hr** |
| 8 | Conduct a post‑mortem review of the initial foothold (credential compromise) and enforce MFA on all privileged accounts. | IAM Administrator | **24 h** |
| 9 | Update endpoint protection policies to block execution of unknown binaries and enforce “run‑as‑least‑privilege”. | Security Engineer | **48 h** |
|10 | Produce a final incident report for senior leadership and regulatory filing (if required). | Incident Commander | **72 h** |

---

**End of Playbooks** – Execute the steps in the order listed, keep all actions logged in the incident ticket, and provide status updates to management at each milestone.
